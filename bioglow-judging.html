1 · Our problem

What is a bloom?

Blue-green algae grow too much. Some make poison.

Not every bloom makes poison, but you can’t tell by looking. So people and dogs should stay out. (NY State DEC)

Our problem

People often find out about a bloom too late.

5 · Our 3D model

The two-light trick

Algae glow. Mud does not!

Two lights and one sensor tell mud, green algae and blue-green algae apart.

8 · Real sensors

The real sensors we need

  • Algae light sensor (sees the glow)
  • A blue light and an orange light
  • Water temperature probe
  • Cloudiness sensor
  • Small computer with Wi-Fi
  • Green / yellow / red light ring
  • Waterproof box and cable seals
  • Foam float and weights

Our goal: the main parts for under $300. Sponsor: to be added.
